Exercise the complete Marketing Studio lifecycle on one real piece of media.

- Read the brief, quality baseline, and up to three relevant prior decisions.
- Produce candidates in an isolated worktree-keyed run directory.
- Catalog candidate checksums in the resource manifest.
- Record why the chosen version was accepted and meaningful alternatives rejected.
- Promote the chosen artifact to a versioned master only after the decision gate.
- Record publication only if a real external platform URL exists.

Done means another agent can reproduce the intent and locate verified evidence
without relying on a local Downloads folder or an untracked fixed filename.
